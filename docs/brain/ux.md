# UX

## Principles

1. **Never ask, guess and let me fix.** Every movement gets a best-guess purpose and category. Low-confidence ones go to
   a **Review** stack (one card at a time, one tap per answer, swipe to skip). No forms on the way in.
2. **One tap actions on any transaction:** *Split*, *For someone*, *Claim*, *Transfer*, *Category*, *Ignore*. The
   choice can apply to the merchant from now on.
3. **Numbers answer questions.** Each screen opens with the answer (free money this month, debt-free in N months), then
   the detail.
4. **Two currencies, never mixed silently.** SAR first, INR beside it; converted totals say "at 1 SAR = ₹x".
5. **Mobile first, thumb reach,** bottom tabs, large tap targets, light and dark.

## Navigation (5 tabs)

| Tab | Answers | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | "How am I doing this month and what's next?" | Free money, salary vs spent vs committed, debt-free date, next 5 dues, alerts, Review count, 12-month look-ahead sparkline |
| **Spending** | "Where did it go?" | Categories, merchants, accounts, transactions, search, trends |
| **People** | "Who owes whom?" | Family (support plan vs actual), friends (balances, settle-up), shared subscriptions, claims to the company |
| **Plan** | "What's coming and can I afford it?" | Commitments (bills, rent, subscriptions, EMIs), funds, 12-month look-ahead, *Can I afford X?*, where to put money, debts and payoff |
| **More** | Everything owned and set up | Accounts & balances, property, vehicles, investments, import statements, devices & phones, Connect Claude, profile, password |

The existing hidden routes (trail, liabilities, buy, balances, health, forecast…) move under these tabs; nothing is
reachable only by URL.
